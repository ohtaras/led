OPPC LED Sign Control

Web Bluetooth control for CoolLEDX / CoolLED1248 flexible LED signs

Your browser doesn't support Web Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer, served over HTTPS or localhost (Web Bluetooth requires a secure context).

Signs

First time with a sign: click "+ Add sign" (top right) to grant access to it. From then on it reconnects automatically on page load whenever it's powered on and in range — no clicking needed. Click a sign's name to rename it. Untick a sign to leave it out of the next action.

Device

Text

Select part of the message, then click a color to paint just that part — rest stays as-is. Click with nothing selected to change color from there onward.
